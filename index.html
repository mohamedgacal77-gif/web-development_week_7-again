    How to Use This Tracker

    Click to show instructions
    1. Enter your Monthly Income and click Save Income.
    2. Fill in the Expense Name, for example, “Groceries”.
    3. Enter the Amount you spent, for example, 500.
    4. Select a Category, such as Food or Transport.
    5. Click Add Expense to save the transaction.
    6. Check your budget summary, category cards, and expense records regularly.

    Tip: Recording expenses frequently makes it easier to manage your budget.
